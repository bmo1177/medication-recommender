Building a Symptom-Based Disease Diagnosis Web App with Flask and Machine Learning

In the age of technology and information, access to accurate and timely healthcare is more critical than ever. With remote healthcare on the rise, we built a symptom-based diagnosis demo — Flask + machine learning, designed to help learners understand how symptom → prediction → guidance pipelines work.

The Problem

People often experience symptoms and want quick, educational answers. Symptoms overlap across diseases, so differentiation is hard. Our goal: a convenient demo where users input symptoms and see a potential condition plus precautions, diet, workouts and medications — for learning only.

The Solution

Users enter comma-separated symptoms. The app encodes them into a 132-length binary vector, runs a pre-trained SVC model, and looks up guidance datasets:

  1. Symptom Input: Friendly form with examples, chips and optional voice input.
  2. ML Model: SVC (`models/svc.pkl`, scikit-learn 1.3.2) trained on symptom ↔ disease data.
  3. Prediction: Instant most-likely disease (41 classes) with severity score.
  4. Guidance: Description, precautions, medications, diet and workouts from CSV datasets.

Key Features

  • User-friendly interface with autocomplete, examples and accessible modals.
  • Educational predictions — clearly labelled, not medical advice.
  • Comprehensive info beyond the label: precautions, diet, workouts.
  • Blog & about for learning how the system works.

Conclusion

By combining Flask, an SVM model and curated guidance data, the demo makes ML-for-health tangible for students. We hope it improves health awareness while keeping safety front and center.

Educational demo only — not a medical device. Consult professionals for medical decisions.
